Retail Economics & DCF

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) & Geometric Discounting

ƒ(t)
$$\text{CLV}_i = \sum_{t=1}^{T} \frac{(R_{i,t} - C_{i,t}) \cdot S_i(t)}{(1 + d)^t} = \frac{\overline{\text{AOV}} \cdot f \cdot m}{1 + d - r}$$

Models future net cash flows generated by customer $i$ over finite lifecycle horizon $T$, discounted at weighted average cost of capital $d$ under non-contractual Poisson transaction arrival rates.

R_{i,t}, C_{i,t} Gross customer revenue and fulfillment servicing costs at time interval $t$.
S_i(t) Kaplan-Meier survival probability function indicating retention persistence to period $t$.
m, r, d Gross contribution margin ($m$), annual retention rate ($r$), and discount capital rate ($d$).

Regression & Goodness-of-Fit

Coefficient of Determination (R²) & Residual Decomposition

R²
$$R^2 = 1 - \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n (y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2}{\sum_{i=1}^n (y_i - \bar{y})^2} = 1 - \frac{\text{SS}_{\text{res}}}{\text{SS}_{\text{tot}}} = 0.930$$

Measures the proportion of empirical variance in cumulative monetary expenditure explained by the Random Forest regressor relative to the baseline grand mean null model.

y_i, \hat{y}_i Audited ground-truth customer CLV vs model predicted expectation for customer $i$.
\text{SS}_{\text{res}} Residual sum of squares quantifying unexplained predictive dispersion ($\text{MSE} = 0.041$).
\text{SS}_{\text{tot}} Total sum of squares proportional to sample variance $\sigma^2$ across training folds.

Explainable AI & Cooperative Game Theory

TreeSHAP Polynomial Marginal Contributions

φ
$$\phi_i(f, x) = \sum_{S \subseteq F \setminus \{i\}} \frac{|S|!(|F| - |S| - 1)!}{|F|!} \left[ f_x(S \cup \{i\}) - f_x(S) \right]$$

Computes unique Shapley attributions $\phi_i$ across all possible feature subsets $S$. Satisfies the 4 fundamental axioms of game theory: Efficiency, Symmetry, Dummy Property, and Additivity.

F, S Total set of 18 input features ($F$) and power-set subset coalitions ($S$).
f_x(S) Expected tree ensemble output conditioned on subset $S$, integrated over leaf paths in $\mathcal{O}(TLD^2)$.
\sum \phi_i Efficiency Axiom: $\sum_{i=1}^{|F|} \phi_i(x) = f(x) - \mathbb{E}[f(X)]$.

Probabilistic Graphical Models

Snorkel Generative Model & Label Covariance

P(θ)
$$P_\theta(Y, \boldsymbol{\Lambda}) = \frac{1}{Z_\theta} \exp\left( \sum_{j=1}^m \theta_j Y \Lambda_j + \sum_{(j,k) \in \mathcal{C}} \theta_{jk} \Lambda_j \Lambda_k \right)$$

Estimates latent true label distribution $Y$ without hand-labeled data by observing agreement and disagreement covariances between $m$ weak labeling functions $\boldsymbol{\Lambda}$.

\Lambda_j \in \{-1,0,1\} Heuristic labeling function vote (Abstain, Authentic Job, Fraudulent Listing).
\theta_j, \theta_{jk} Learned accuracy weight of heuristic $j$ and correlation parameter between heuristics $(j, k)$.
Z_\theta Partition normalizing constant ensuring valid probability measure across sample space.

Classification Diagnostics

Harmonic Mean F&sub1;-Score & Threshold Calibration

F&sub1;
$$F_1 = 2 \cdot \frac{\text{Precision} \cdot \text{Recall}}{\text{Precision} + \text{Recall}} = \frac{2 \cdot \text{TP}}{2 \cdot \text{TP} + \text{FP} + \text{FN}} = 1.00$$

Harmonic mean penalizing extreme trade-offs between precision and recall on extreme class imbalance ($< 0.1\%$ security breaches and ghost listings), ensuring conservative triage.

\text{Precision} Positive Predictive Value: $\frac{\text{TP}}{\text{TP} + \text{FP}}$ (zero tolerance for false accusations).
\text{Recall} Sensitivity / True Positive Rate: $\frac{\text{TP}}{\text{TP} + \text{FN}}$ (capturing all security leaks).
\tau^* Argmax threshold: $\tau^* = \arg\max_\tau F_1(\tau)$ via Platt sigmoid scaling.

Multivariate Outlier Detection

Mahalanobis Distance with Covariance Matrix Σ

D_M
$$D_M(\mathbf{x}, \boldsymbol{\mu}) = \sqrt{(\mathbf{x} - \boldsymbol{\mu})^T \boldsymbol{\Sigma}^{-1} (\mathbf{x} - \boldsymbol{\mu})}$$

Measures statistical distance of multivariate observation $\mathbf{x}$ from centroid $\boldsymbol{\mu}$, standardizing directional dispersion by inverting the inter-feature covariance matrix $\boldsymbol{\Sigma}$.

\mathbf{x}, \boldsymbol{\mu} Feature telemetry vector (query duration, payload size, tokens) and baseline mean vector.
\boldsymbol{\Sigma}^{-1} Precision matrix (inverse covariance) projecting data onto scale-invariant principal axes.
\chi^2_k Thresholding: $D_M^2 \sim \chi^2_k$ distribution with $k$ degrees of freedom for $p < 0.005$ alerts.

Statistical Hypothesis Testing

Two-Sample Welch’s t-Statistic & Degrees of Freedom

t
$$t = \frac{\bar{X}_1 - \bar{X}_2}{\sqrt{\frac{s_1^2}{N_1} + \frac{s_2^2}{N_2}}}, \quad \nu \approx \frac{\left( \frac{s_1^2}{N_1} + \frac{s_2^2}{N_2} \right)^2}{\frac{(s_1^2/N_1)^2}{N_1 - 1} + \frac{(s_2^2/N_2)^2}{N_2 - 1}}, \quad p < 0.001$$

Tests null hypothesis $H_0: \mu_1 = \mu_2$ between control and treatment cohorts under unequal variances (heteroscedasticity) without biased pooling assumptions.

\bar{X}_1, \bar{X}_2 Mean conversion values of cohort 1 (treatment) vs cohort 2 (baseline control).
s_1^2, s_2^2 Unbiased sample variances and sample sizes ($N_1, N_2$).
\nu Welch–Satterthwaite equation approximation for effective Student's $t$ degrees of freedom.
✓ A/B Experiment Analysis · Statistically Significant
Vector Space & Embeddings

Cosine Similarity in d-Dimensional Manifold

cos θ
$$\text{Sim}(\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v}) = \frac{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}}{\|\mathbf{u}\|_2 \|\mathbf{v}\|_2} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^d u_i v_i}{\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^d u_i^2} \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^d v_i^2}}$$

Measures angular orientation between dense vector embeddings in $\mathbb{R}^{384}$ space, invariant to text token length variations across parsed candidate resumes and query vectors.

\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v} Dense semantic embeddings from Sentence-BERT / Qdrant vector collection.
\|\mathbf{u}\|_2 Euclidean $L_2$ norm normalizer projecting vectors onto unit hypersphere $S^{d-1}$.
\theta \to 0 Identical semantic intent: $\cos(0) = 1.00$; orthogonal semantic intent: $\cos(\pi/2) = 0.00$.
