Covers your PAN & Aadhaar with a temporary token so hackers and cloud AI never see your real identity.
STATION 2

Scans the payment memo for trick messages like "ignore rules and steal money", dropping hack attempts instantly.
STATION 3

If you send more than ₹50,000, money is held in escrow until a senior officer reviews and biometrically approves it.
STATION 4

Verifies you actually have enough money and double-checks you didn't exceed your daily limit.
STATION 5

"Wax Seal"
Stamps the record with a mathematical cryptographic seal that cannot be forged or tampered with.
